## 英文名称

Lizhang

## 英文短简介

Track gifts given and received, manage contacts, and keep a local gift ledger.

## 英文完整简介

Lizhang is a local-first gift-money ledger. No account is required. Record gifts given and received, including contacts, amounts, dates, occasions, and notes. Browse your calendar, search records, and review gift statistics.

Manage contacts manually, or choose contacts to import after granting permission. Create independent date reminders with one-time or annual recurrence. Notification delivery depends on device permissions, system scheduling, and background restrictions.

Export gift records to CSV or Excel for viewing and organizing. Create a regular local backup or choose a password-encrypted backup. Regular backups, CSV files, and Excel files are not encrypted. Encrypted backups require the original password; the app and developer cannot recover it or decrypt the backup for you.

Backups include contacts and gift records. They exclude independent reminders, reminder settings, themes, language preferences, onboarding progress, and practice data. Restoring a backup replaces the current contacts and gift records rather than merging them, and cannot be undone within the app. Save a backup of your current ledger first.

Lizhang does not provide online accounts, a cloud ledger, automatic cloud sync, or remote account recovery. If you choose a third-party cloud storage provider for exported files or backups, that provider handles those files under its own rules. Keep your device, backup files, and passwords secure.
